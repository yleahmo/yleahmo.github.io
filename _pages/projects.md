# Selected Projects

## Arctic Housing, Energy & Community Resilience

Research examining housing conditions, energy systems, climate adaptation, and community priorities in rural Alaska through an NSF Navigating the New Arctic project.

## Human-Centered Smart Buildings

Research integrating occupant behavior, sensing, building performance modeling, and intelligent building systems to improve energy efficiency and decision-making.

## Construction Safety with VR & AI

Immersive construction safety research and workforce development using Virtual Reality and AI-enabled approaches, including collaboration with CIRAS.

## Student-Designed Immersive Learning

Research supported through the Miller Faculty Fellowship investigating student-designed VR experiences as an approach to active learning in engineering education.

## Healthy Buildings & Indoor Environmental Quality

Research examining indoor environmental quality, housing conditions, occupant decision-making, and emerging health-related built-environment challenges.
