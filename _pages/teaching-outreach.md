# Teaching & Outreach

B³ Lab integrates research, education, and engagement through immersive learning, engineering education, workforce development, and community outreach.

## Engineering Education

Research and educational initiatives incorporating BIM, VR, digital technologies, and student-designed immersive experiences into engineering courses.

## Workforce Development

Immersive and technology-enhanced training approaches for the architecture, engineering, and construction workforce.

## Community Engagement

Collaborative research and outreach activities connecting built-environment research with communities, educators, industry, and public-sector partners.
