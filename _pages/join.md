# Join B³ Lab

B³ Lab welcomes motivated students interested in human-centered research at the intersection of the built environment, human behavior, and emerging technologies.

Prospective students with interests in building energy, occupant behavior, smart buildings, digital twins, artificial intelligence, BIM, immersive technologies, construction safety, or healthy and sustainable built environments are encouraged to explore our research.
