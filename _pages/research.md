# Research

B³ Lab advances human-centered intelligence for the built environment by investigating interactions among people, buildings, and emerging technologies.

## Human-Building Interaction & Occupant Behavior

Research on how occupants perceive, interact with, and make decisions within buildings, with applications to energy use, environmental performance, technology adoption, and building operation.

## Intelligent Buildings & Digital Twins

Development of data-driven and digital twin approaches for building performance monitoring, prediction, decision support, and intelligent building management.

## AI & Data-Driven Built Environments

Application of artificial intelligence and data-driven methods, including machine learning, natural language processing, and large language models, to building and construction problems.

## Healthy, Sustainable & Resilient Built Environments

Research addressing building energy, indoor environmental quality, housing, climate adaptation, community resilience, and human well-being.

## Immersive Technologies & Human-Centered Learning

Use of Virtual and Augmented Reality, Building Information Modeling, and other emerging technologies for construction safety, engineering education, workforce training, and human-building interaction research.
